UrbanTherm is a hyperlocal urban heat intelligence prototype for Ghaziabad, combining satellite-derived land surface temperature, vegetation and built-up indicators with environmental data and machine learning to understand heat risk at a fine spatial scale.

01 · THE IDEA

Why UrbanTherm?

City-wide averages can hide the places where heat is concentrated.

Heat is not evenly distributed.

Two nearby areas can experience very different thermal conditions because of differences in vegetation, built-up surfaces and local environment. UrbanTherm turns that spatial variation into an explorable grid-based view.

From pixels to decisions.

The system processes a 500m × 500m grid across the Ghaziabad study area. Each cell can be inspected individually through the dashboard, heat map and AI prediction interface.

02 · PIPELINE

How UrbanTherm Works

A four-stage pipeline turns raw environmental observations into an interactive heat-risk view.

◉
01 · OBSERVE

Satellite Data

Landsat imagery provides spectral and thermal information for the study area, including red, near-infrared, shortwave-infrared and thermal bands.

≈
02 · ENGINEER

Environmental Features

Satellite signals are transformed into indicators such as NDVI, NDBI and land surface temperature, then combined with weather features.

⌁
03 · ANALYZE

Machine Learning

A Random Forest classifier uses environmental features to estimate the heat-risk category for an individual grid cell.

✦
04 · EXPLORE

Urban Intelligence

Results are exposed through interactive maps, grid-level inspection and an AI prediction interface so spatial patterns can be explored visually.

03 · THE SIGNALS

What the Model Sees

UrbanTherm connects thermal conditions with the physical characteristics of the urban surface.

◌

Land Surface Temperature

Represents the observed thermal state of the land surface derived from satellite thermal data.

⌁

Vegetation · NDVI

Provides a vegetation-related signal that helps distinguish greener and less vegetated urban surfaces.

▦

Built-up · NDBI

Provides a built-up surface indicator that helps characterize the urban fabric around each grid cell.
